Arena

Arena size grows with the number of players. Generated maps come in rectangles, hexagons, octagons, triangles, trapezoids and pentagons.

Free for all: everyone fights everyone. Lose a round and you pick an upgrade card; first to the target score wins.

Teams on: up to 8 teams. Set each player's team in the list. The losing team picks cards.

A huge map of themed zones. Space jumps out of the flying bus and opens your parachute. Colored items give a card of that rarity. E gets on or off a scooter, bike or car. Tab switches first / third person.

The final wave brings a boss. You get one free retry (loss token) per run, not on the boss wave. Beat the boss to keep going with harder waves. Co-op never uses Lava Lake.

A flat battlefield with a core block in the middle. Enemies from every mode walk in from the edges and go for the core; aggressive ones (and any that get a player or turret in reach) come for you first. Blocking works as usual. Press E (use) to pick up and put down the blocks, cubes and turrets that parachute in; you can't shoot while carrying one. Each wave starts with 60 seconds to build. Every 3rd wave drops an upgrade: grab it and everyone picks a card after the wave. You respawn after a timer that grows with each death that wave. If the core falls, retry the wave or start over.

Autobattler. In the store, buy unit groups and tactic cards (same price for every offer) and arrange them on your 5x5 board: tactic cards boost the squares they reach, chess style. Upgrade the store for more offers and better tiers; its price drops every battle. Each player's board fights its own copy of the wave and survivors move on to other fields. Enemies left standing cost team health. Round 20: Amon. Bots in the lobby play their own boards.

2D: A / D move, W or Space jumps, the mouse aims anywhere. The arena edges hurt: block as you touch one to bounce off it. Falling into a pit is a knockout.

Smash style on 3D floating stages. Hits raise your damage %, and the higher it is the farther you fly. Get knocked past the edge of the screen and you lose a stock. A / D move, W or Space jumps (jump again in the air), the mouse aims. No card picks: your Character cosmetic is your fighter, each with its own fixed build. Bots take the other fighters.

The Wiki · card tools

Every card is a folder inside cards/ holding a card.json and an optional picture (art.png, 700×500 works well). Copy cards/_TEMPLATE, rename the folder, edit the JSON, then press Reload cards. Folders starting with _ are ignored.

{
  "id": "my-card",            // unique, lowercase-with-dashes
  "name": "My Card",
  "rarity": "common",         // common | uncommon | rare
  "icon": "⭐",               // shown if there's no picture
  "description": "One sentence about what it does.",
  "image": "art.png",
  "modifiers": [
    { "stat": "damage", "op": "mul", "value": 1.25 },  // ×1.25 = +25%
    { "stat": "ammo",   "op": "add", "value": 1 }      // +1
  ]
}
